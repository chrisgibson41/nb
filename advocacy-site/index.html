The Mission

1 in 10 women live with endometriosis — and far too many spend years being dismissed, misdiagnosed, and told the pain is in their head.

I'm a patient advocate working to change that. What began as leading a support group in Glasgow has grown into a platform: sharing my story on national television and radio, coaching organisations on how to support employees with chronic illness, and taking the lived experience of patients directly to those who write the policy.

My work sits at the meeting point of community and change — holding space for people who are hurting, while pushing the systems around them to do better.
